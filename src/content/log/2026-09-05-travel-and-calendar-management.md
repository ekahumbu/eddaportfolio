I practiced calendar and travel management on two scenarios: a CEO's week rebuilt after nine conflicting requests, and a [two-stop business trip](/tasks/two-city-trip-plan) planned within a $1,600 budget. Both taught me the same thing: write down the person's rules first, then judge every option against them. That is how 18 calendar clashes became 14 documented decisions, and how the trip came in at $1,466 with a fallback plan ready. Next, I'll run my pre-send checklist before the first review, not after it.
